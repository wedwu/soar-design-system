# Slots

A **slot** is a named place inside a component where you put your own content. It's how you give a button an icon, a card a header, or a dialog its footer buttons, without the component needing a property for every possibility.

## How they work

Every component has a **default slot**. Anything you put inside the tag without a `slot` attribute goes there:

```html
<wa-button>Save</wa-button>   <!-- "Save" goes into the default slot -->
```

**Named slots** take content marked with a matching `slot` attribute:

```html
<wa-button variant="brand">
  <wa-icon slot="start" name="floppy-disk"></wa-icon>   <!-- before the label -->
  Save
  <wa-icon slot="end" name="arrow-right"></wa-icon>     <!-- after the label -->
</wa-button>

<wa-card>
  <div slot="header">Usage this month</div>
  You've used 62% of your quota.
  <div slot="footer"><wa-button>Upgrade</wa-button></div>
</wa-card>
```

## Rules of thumb

- **`slot` goes on a direct child.** `<div><span slot="header">` does not fill the header. The `slot` attribute only counts on the component's own children.
- **Many slots have an attribute shortcut.** For example, `label` on `<wa-input>` or `summary` on `<wa-details>`. Use the attribute for plain text, and the slot when you need markup such as a link or an icon.
- **You style slotted content normally.** It stays in your page, not inside the component, so your app's CSS applies to it as usual.
- **An empty slot takes no space.** Components hide a header, footer or icon area when nothing is slotted into it.
- **Frameworks pass `slot` through as-is.** In React, Vue and Angular, write `slot="footer"` on the child element exactly as in HTML.
